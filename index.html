Our Story

Born from the
streets of Dhaka

SOLÈME was born at the intersection of ambition and identity. In a city where streets pulse with a unique energy — where rickshaws share lanes with startups, and tradition walks beside transformation — we saw a gap.

International sneaker brands didn't speak to us. We needed something that understood our heat, our streets, our pride. SOLÈME is the answer: premium footwear built for South Asian lives, priced for South Asian realities.

Brand Positioning

What makes
SOLÈME different

01
◈

Premium Without the Premium Tax

Global sneaker brands charge 3–5× for import duties and brand markup. We eliminate the chain and pass the value to you. Quality leather, EVA soles, breathable mesh — priced honestly.

02
◉

Designed for Our Climate

Dhaka's heat, humidity, and monsoon rains demand footwear engineered for them. SOLÈME soles feature anti-slip rubber compounds tested on our streets, not European sidewalks.

03

Size-Inclusive for South Asia

Western brands often exclude sizes 35–46 that fit most Bangladeshi feet. Every SOLÈME silhouette comes in full-range sizing. Half-sizes available on select models.

04

Local Culture, Global Aesthetics

Our colorways draw from rickshaw art, jamdani weaving, and terracotta pottery. Street-ready silhouettes with design DNA rooted in Bangladesh — visible to those who know, subtle to those who don't.
