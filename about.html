About

Hello, I'm
Sabbir.

I believe the best moments deserve a calm place to live — away from the endless scroll. This site is that place.

I'm Sabbir Shikder — born and raised in Manikganj, and now living in the heart of Dhaka, Bangladesh. I love capturing everyday life through my camera: the people I meet, the places I wander, and the quiet details that are easy to miss.

This page is my own corner of the web, where I gather my favourite photographs and share the short stories behind them. Take a look around, and feel free to say hello on Facebook.
